# Workplace communication

## Training
**Tok Pisin:** Gutpela moning, brother. Long dispela wik, tupela man i mas go long trening. H bai go long trening bilong paia trak long Wenesde, na X bai go long trening bilong liklik kar long Sarere.

**English:** Good morning, brother. Two people need training this week. H will attend fire truck training on Wednesday, and X will attend light vehicle training on Saturday.

## Shift handover photo request
**Tok Pisin:** Halo olgeta. Tumora em Sande. Planti manmeri bai stap long taim bilong senisim shift. Em gutpela taim long kisim ol piksa na video bilong handover. Plis bihainim tok bilong ol supervisor na team leader, na salim ol piksa na video long grup bihain long miting. Tenkyu tru.

## Safety / progress
**Tok Pisin:** Tude yumi mas wok hariap moa. Bos bilong Refinery i no amamas long progress bilong wok. Em i ting wok i go isi tumas.

**English:** We need to speed up today. The Refinery Manager is dissatisfied with our progress and believes the work is moving too slowly.

## Device registration
**Tok Pisin:** Taim yu konekt long network pinis, plis tok save long mipela. Mipela bai rejistarim device bilong yu. Ol device mipela i no save long en inap lusim network access. Mipela i mekim olsem bilong lukautim gutpela network bilong olgeta.

## Purchasing terms
**Tok Pisin:** Yu ken makim samting na salim link i kam. Mi bai sekim prais na service fee, na tok save long yu long total cost. Mi mas kisim peimen pastaim bipo mi putim oda. 

**English:** Choose the item and send me the link. I'll confirm the price, service fee, and total. I must receive payment before placing the order.

Preserve the user's intended conditionality, quantities and payment sequence. Do not translate 'we may consider' as a definite threat.
