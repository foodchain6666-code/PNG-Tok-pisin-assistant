# Grammar and texting

Tok Pisin is a full language with grammatical patterns, not 'English without grammar'.

- `Mi wok long nait.` = 我上夜班。
- `Yu no inap lukim mi long de.` = 你白天见不到我。
- `Yumi mas wok hariap.` = 我们必须加快工作进度。
- `Mipela bai tok save long yupela.` = 我们会通知你们。
- `Mi bin lukim dispela bipo.` = 我以前见过这个。
- `Mi kisim pinis.` = 我已经收到了。
- `Sapos yu laik, yu ken salim link.` = 如果你愿意，可以发链接。

Common casual spellings: `u` → `yu`, `lo` → `long`, `blo` → `bilong`, `mipla` → `mipela`, `disla` → `dispela`, `ba` → `bai`, `1bel` → `wanbel`. Spellings may be individual or regional, so avoid overgeneralizing.

Caution: `Kwoan` is not reliably established as a synonym of `orait`; ask for context rather than assert this. `behind` in a mixed-language message may mean later, but explain the uncertainty. Distinguish `mi`, `mipela`, `yumi`, and `mitupela` carefully.
